Service Sunset Notice

Thank You for The Journey

Roblox Chat Launcher will officially sunset its hosted services after December 31st, 2026. We want to express our deepest gratitude to everyone who used, supported, and contributed to the platform.

Hosted services sunset December 31, 2026
Open Source

Source Code Remains Free

The source code will remain open and free for all to view, use, modify, and deploy under the GPL-3.0 license. You can freely inspect and self-host the project anytime.

Data Privacy

Permanent Data Removal

You can type /unverify in the client at any time before December 31, 2026 to permanently and irreversibly wipe your data from our database. Additionally, all remaining database tables will be permanently dropped within 30 days of the sunset date.

Uninstallation

How to Uninstall

You can easily remove the application anytime through your system's Add or Remove Programs menu. Doing so will automatically restore your original Roblox client or bootstrapper as your default application.

The Road Ahead

What Happens Next?

Here's what the sunset means for Roblox Chat Launcher and its users.

01

Until December 31

Hosted Roblox Chat Launcher services will continue operating while the project approaches its sunset date.

02

December 31, 2026

Hosted services will be discontinued. The hosted platform should no longer be relied upon after this date.

03

After the Sunset

The source code will remain available under the GPL-3.0 license for anyone who wants to inspect, modify, or self-host it.

Open Source

Want to Keep It Running?

Roblox Chat Launcher will remain open source under the GPL-3.0 license. If you have the technical ability and infrastructure, you can continue the project independently by self-hosting it. You will also need to swap out the server-side moderation service and local-side message filter.

Please note that self-hosting is not operated or supported by the Roblox Chat Launcher hosted service.

Dear Community,

When I first set out to build Roblox Chat Launcher, my goal was simple: to bring back the secure communication that Roblox took away from us, without requiring your facial data or government ID just to communicate with teammates.

At first, the project was just a fun hobby that I never thought anyone would use. Looking back at how far the project has come, words cannot express how proud and grateful I am to the users who helped make this service possible. The fact that I can't even say a concrete number of guest users who've used Roblox Chat Launcher goes to show that we've achieved our core mission of privacy.

Thank you for every bug report, every pull request, every kind message, and for trusting us along the way. You made this journey truly unforgettable.

With gratitude,
